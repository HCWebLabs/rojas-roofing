Storm damage, step by step

How a roof insurance claim actually works

If a storm just came through, the claim process can feel like a second storm. Here's the whole thing in order, so you know what happens next at every point.

  1. Document the damage

    Photograph from the ground and note the storm date. Don't climb up — that's our job. Interior water stains count too.

  2. Get a free inspection

    We inspect and tell you honestly whether the damage is worth a claim. If it isn't, we'll say so and quote the repair instead.

  3. File with your insurer

    You call your insurance company to open the claim — only you can do this part. They'll assign an adjuster and a claim number.

  4. We meet your adjuster

    We're on the roof with the adjuster during their visit, pointing out every item of documented damage so nothing gets missed.

  5. Review the approval together

    When the scope and payment letter arrives, we sit down and translate it — what's covered, your deductible, and what isn't included.

  6. Build day & walkthrough

    Most replacements finish in a day. We walk it with you, run a magnetic nail sweep, and hand over your warranty paperwork.

One honest note: your deductible is your deductible. Any roofer who offers to "cover" or "waive" it is describing insurance fraud, and it can void your claim. We won't do it — and you shouldn't sign with anyone who will.
